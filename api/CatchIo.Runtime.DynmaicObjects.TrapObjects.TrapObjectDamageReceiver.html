    TakeDamage(IDamage)

    Overrides the TakeDamage method to apply direct damage to the trap object. This method handles two types of damage: FixedDamage and PercentageDamage.

    • FixedDamage applies a constant amount of damage.
    • PercentageDamage applies a percentage of the trap's current health as damage. If the trap's health is zero or below, no damage is applied.
